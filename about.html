Maisha Mastora

Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering · University of New Hampshire

I am a PhD student at the University of New Hampshire, advised by Dr. Dean Sullivan, and I expect to graduate in summer 2027. My research covers automated program repair for hardware and firmware, with large language model evaluation as the common thread. I work at the RTL, HLS, and firmware levels, and I try to make sure the benchmarks and oracles behind my results can be trusted.
